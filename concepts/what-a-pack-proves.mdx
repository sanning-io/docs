---
title: "What a pack proves"
description: "What a verified pack of agent records proves, and what it does not"
---

A verified pack proves that the holder of a signing key signed exact records, that the
records follow the `sanning.agent/1` vocabulary, and that the records were in the set a
stamp witnessed. It does not prove that what the records say happened. Verification
runs offline, on the checker's machine, and Sanning is never in the trust path.

## What it proves

A verified `agent.*` record proves four things:

- The holder of the signing key signed exactly these bytes.
- The record is well formed: its type is one of the [17 event types](/reference/event-types),
  and every field is one the vocabulary names, in the shape it names.
- `event.content_hash` commits to one exact byte string, so anyone later handed the
  content can confirm it is what the agent recorded.
- On a chain, nobody removed or reordered a record between two links.

## What it does not prove

- **That what the record says happened.** Signed is not true. A record is the agent's
  own account, written by an adapter inside the agent's process.
- **That the adapter saw every step.** A step the framework never reported has no
  record. The adapter's `capture.not_captured` list is the adapter's own word.
- **That an operation with no end failed.** A start with no end says only that no end
  record was written.
- **That a revealed field matches the content.** The two agree only where a verifier
  holds the content and compares them.
- **That a hidden field cannot be recovered.** A hidden field keeps the hash of its
  value, and anyone who guesses a short value can confirm the guess.
- **That a clear field carries no customer data.** The schema bounds each field's shape
  and length, never its meaning.
- **That a scrubbed content hash matches the framework's raw object.** Where
  `context.content_scrubbed` is `true`, the hash commits to the object after the adapter
  replaced credential-shaped values.
- **What Sanning received or stored.** `context.data_mode` is the agent's statement of
  what it sent. Ids reach Sanning in clear.
- **That a closing record lists every record of its invocation.** Completeness is a
  separate question that the vocabulary does not answer.

For the verdict words and the limits on a whole pack, see
[What a verdict means](/verify/what-a-verdict-means). For the argument that nobody
has to trust Sanning, see [The trust boundary](/concepts/trust-boundary).
